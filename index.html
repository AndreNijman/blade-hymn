Click a stage once to select it and again to start. Hold Esc for two seconds to return to the menu. The speedrun clock starts when a stage has faded in and stops when its last enemy falls.

Blade Hymn is made by Eason (source), who designed and built the game in Python/Pygame. This is an unofficial browser port of that game, hosted with permission.
